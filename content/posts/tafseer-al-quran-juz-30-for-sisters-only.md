*Join over 200 sisters in our Tafsir Juz Amma course. We start with a new Surah today, inshaAllah!*

[**Tafsir Juz Amma**](/tafseer-al-quran/) is an online course presented by Arriqaaq to non-arabic speaking Muslims and new reverts. The motive of this course is to understand the Quran in the light of Quran and Sunnah, by following the understanding and teachings of the righteous predecessors (salaf us saliheen). The course will be taught based on a number of tafseers like ***Tafseer ibn Katheer***, ***Tafseer al Baghawi*** and ***Tafseer As-Sa'di***.

###   
**Learning outcomes:**

\- Understand the Quran in accordance to the understanding of the salaf  
\- Learn from the well renowned tafseers of Ibn Katheer, Baghawi and Shaykh Nasir as Sadi rahimahullah  
\- Become familiar with the principles of tafseer  
\- Understand the fiqh of an ayah  

### **Instructor:**

###   
**About The Teacher**

Ustadha Umm Amrah Noora Jukaku is a graduate in Sharee'ah and Usool ud Deen from University of Sharjah. She learnt hadith and its sciences under Dr. Awaad Khalaf. Tafseer under Dr. Eyadha al-Kobaisi, Dr Mustafa Mashni  and Dr Afaf Ahmed.  She studied Fiqh and Usool Al Fiqh under  Dr. Hasan ar-Rifaee, Aqeedah under Dr. Ahmad Muadh Haqqi, Nahw and Sarf under Dr. Abdul Qadir as-Sadi, Balagah under Dr Mustafa Mashni. She also benefited from Dr.Abdullah al-Bukhari of Madeenah university, Shaykh Sulayman ar-Ruhaylee and Shaykh Sultan Al Eid.
